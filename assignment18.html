How to Measure

  • Chest: Measure around the fullest part of the chest.
  • Shoulder: Measure from one shoulder edge to the other.
  • Length: Measure from the top of the shoulder to the bottom.
  • Sleeve: Measure from the shoulder seam to the sleeve end.
